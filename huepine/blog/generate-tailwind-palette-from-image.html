How to Generate a Tailwind Color Palette From an Image (No Upload, No Signup)

October 1, 2026

You have a photo, a product shot, or a moodboard, and you want its colors living in your tailwind.config by the end of the next five minutes. Most tools make you pick between two annoyances: upload the image to someone's server, or sign up for an account before you can copy a single hex code. Here's how to skip both and get a Tailwind-ready palette straight from any image, entirely in your browser.

The usual friction

Search "Tailwind palette from image" and you'll find plenty of generators. They work, but the common pattern is: drag your image into an uploader, wait for it to process on a server, then hit a wall when you want the actual config — that part is often gated behind a login or a Pro plan. For a quick personal project that's fine. For client work, an unreleased product screenshot, or anything under NDA, uploading the source image to a third party is a non-starter.

That's the gap Huepine fills: the extraction happens on your machine, the image never leaves the browser, and there's no account to create.

Step 1 — Open the image locally

Go to the Huepine app and drop in (or open) a local image — a photo, a screenshot, a moodboard, whatever has the colors you want. Nothing uploads. The decoding and color sampling run client-side using the browser canvas, so your file stays on your device. That alone makes it safe to point at confidential design work.

Step 2 — Extract the dominant colors

Huepine uses median-cut sampling to pull the dominant colors out of the image and sorts them by luminance, so you get a clean light-to-dark run instead of a random jumble. You choose how many colors you want — anywhere from 3 to 10 — depending on whether you need a tight brand set or a fuller palette to work from.

Click any swatch to copy its HEX instantly. This part — the extraction, the on-screen palette, and copying individual hex codes — is free. No gate, no watermark on the colors themselves.

Step 3 — Check your harmonies

A raw palette pulled from a photo isn't always a balanced UI palette. Click any swatch to set it as the base and Huepine generates complementary, analogous, and triadic sets from it. This is the fast way to sanity-check an accent color or find a secondary that actually works against your dominant tone before you commit it to a config.

Step 4 — Export a Tailwind config

When the palette looks right, open the Export panel. Huepine turns your extracted palette into a Tailwind color config you can paste straight into your project — named color keys with their hex values, ready to extend theme.colors. If you'd rather work in plain CSS custom properties, SCSS variables, or JSON (with HEX / RGB / HSL for each color), those exports are there too.

Paste it under theme.extend.colors, and your extracted palette is live as utility classes. No manual copy-pasting hex codes one at a time.

Straight talk on pricing: the code exports — Tailwind, CSS, SCSS, JSON — unlock with a one-time lifetime license. There's no subscription and no account; you pay once and the dev-ready output is yours. The on-screen palette, the harmonies, individual hex copying, and a labeled PNG palette strip stay free. So you can confirm Huepine pulls exactly the colors you want before you ever pay, and the paid part is the thing that actually saves you time: clean, pasteable code.

What Huepine deliberately doesn't do

Honesty keeps you from being disappointed later. Huepine works on one image at a time — there's no batch processing, no cloud sync, and no saved palette library or account. That's a choice, not an oversight: it's built to be fast and local, open-and-go, rather than another dashboard to log into. If you need a team workspace with shared palette history, Huepine isn't that tool. If you need to turn an image into a Tailwind config in under a minute without uploading anything, it is.

Why do it this way

  • Nothing uploaded — the image is decoded and sampled in your browser, which matters for client and NDA work.
  • No signup — open the app and start; there's no login between you and your palette.
  • Dev-ready output — real, pasteable Tailwind / CSS / SCSS / JSON, not just pretty swatches you have to transcribe by hand.
  • Pay once — a lifetime license, no recurring subscription.
  • Runs anywhere — any modern browser, any device.

The workflow is short on purpose: drop an image, pick your colors, check the harmonies, export the config. Then get back to building.
